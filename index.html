Institutional Legacy

Why Recruit from NCE Chandi?

Inaugurated on November 19, 2008, Nalanda College of Engineering operates under the Department of Science, Technology and Technical Education, Govt. of Bihar, and is affiliated with Bihar Engineering University.

Modern Academic Programs

Offering B.Tech in CSE, AI & ML, Aeronautical, Civil, Electrical & Electronics, and Mechanical Engineering, alongside advanced M.Tech programs in Power Systems and CSE.

State-of-the-Art Labs

A 51.68-acre campus housing a modern Computer Centre with 180+ LAN-connected high-speed systems, departmental libraries, and advanced research workshops.

Holistic Development

Students lead technical innovation via the Coding Club, Startup/E-Cell, Spark Photography, Atisa Tech Fest, and civil services prep initiatives.
